# 1. Product Overview

TWO is a modern billing workspace built for freelancers, creators, vendors, and small-to-medium business owners to generate invoices, track payment statuses, convert invoices into receipts, and manage client financial records from a single intuitive platform.

The product is designed to simplify financial documentation by providing a fast, responsive, and trustworthy invoice-to-receipt workflow that eliminates manual record-keeping inefficiencies.

TWO focuses on:

- Speed
- Simplicity
- Professionalism
- Mobile-first usability
- Financial clarity

This MVP is designed to validate whether an intuitive billing system can significantly improve financial workflow efficiency for small businesses.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Many freelancers and SMEs struggle with fragmented and inefficient billing systems.

Common challenges include:

## Manual Documentation
Invoices are often created manually using generic templates or messaging platforms.

## Poor Payment Tracking
Users lack visibility into payment statuses and overdue invoices.

## Disorganized Client Records
Client information is often scattered across different tools.

## Delayed Receipt Generation
Receipts are created separately after payments are made.

## Lack of Professional Branding
Small businesses often send unstructured financial documentation.

These inefficiencies reduce trust, delay payments, and increase administrative burden.

# 8. Edge Cases

The product must handle:

## Empty Dashboard
Display onboarding prompts.

---

## Duplicate Invoice IDs
Automatically generate unique IDs.

---

## Missing Required Fields
Prevent submission with clear validation.

---

## Invalid Pricing Inputs
Restrict invalid numeric values.

---

## Failed PDF Export
Display retry option.

---

## No Client Records
Prompt user to create a client.

---

## Overdue Invoice Logic
Automatically update status.

---

## Mobile Layout Constraints
Reflow invoice builder intelligently.
